Let’s play.

  1. Make two teams.

    Use Teams & settings to name them. Click a team’s card to let that team choose the next question.

  2. Pick a category and point value.

    Read the question aloud. Start the optional timer whenever everyone is ready.

  3. Choose the outcome.

    No answer passes the next pick to the other team. Incorrect answer gives the other team a steal on the same question. Correct answer awards the points and lets the answering team pick again. Each completed question shows the correct answer first; click Next category when you’re ready.

  4. Check answers when you’re ready.

    Reveal answer is separate from the outcome buttons. A first incorrect answer keeps the answer hidden for the steal. After a correct answer, no answer, or failed steal, review the answer and Scripture reference, then click Next category. If the steal misses or passes, the original team picks next. Undo fixes the last completed question, score, and turn.

Double Jeopardy & wagers

Use the round buttons to switch boards with scores carried over. The updated Bible Basics set has two boards and a Final question. Mark Daily Doubles in the question editor: one in Round 1 and two in Double Jeopardy. A team locks its Daily Double wager before seeing the question; only that team answers. Wagers are won or lost even when ordinary answer deductions are off.

In this youth game, Daily Double wagers may be 0 up to the larger of the team’s score or the highest clue value on that board. Final Jeopardy wagers may be 0 up to the team’s positive score. A team at zero or below can play Final with a zero wager. Both Final wagers are masked and locked before the question appears. There are no steals on wagering questions.

Use Print Q&A on the board for a host answer sheet of every configured round, then print it or save it as a PDF before the game.

Edit questions directly, then download a game file to reuse it later. Your current game, team names, and progress save in this browser when storage is available. Use the same browser and address to resume.
